Job market paper

Online Learning Algorithms for Replenishment and Rationing in Two-Class Inventory Systems

Xinxiu Wang, Cong Shi, and Xin Geng

Abstract +

Firms with scheduled delivery commitments must decide how much inventory to reserve and how much to make available to other customers. When unmet demand is unrecorded, these decisions also limit what a firm can learn. We study joint replenishment and rationing with fixed ordering costs and censored demand. Our approach reconstructs the performance of multiple candidate policies from a common stream of sales observations and adaptively eliminates inferior policies. We establish a regret guarantee and justify the structured policy class used as the learning benchmark.

Working paper

Online Learning for Dynamic Pricing with Endogenous Reviews

Xinxiu Wang

Abstract +

I study a seller who learns how prices and ratings affect demand, while the prices customers pay also affect the reviews they leave. Price experiments therefore change both the seller's estimates and the rating record that future buyers see. I develop a learning policy that accounts for these lasting effects of exploration, establish a regret guarantee relative to the full-information stochastic optimum, and study a batched version that reduces the frequency of price changes.

Working paper

Direct Selling under Yield Uncertainty and Its Impact on Food Waste

Xinxiu Wang and Xin Geng

Abstract +

Direct selling offers an additional outlet for farm output, but it can also change production incentives and a retailer's procurement decisions. We examine these responses under yield uncertainty and different timings of a farmer's commitment to activate a direct channel. An additional sales outlet need not reduce food waste: increased production or altered procurement may offset its benefits. The analysis shows how the timing of channel commitments affects the environmental consequences of direct selling.

Work in progress

Managing Supply Chain Responsibility: The Roles of Channel Structure and Collaborative Effort

Xinxiu Wang and Sammi Yu Tang

Overview +

Buyers often help suppliers improve responsibility practices by sharing investment costs. We examine how this collaboration changes when a supplier also sells directly to consumers. Direct selling can strengthen a supplier's incentive to invest by increasing its exposure to the consequences of a violation, while a buyer's reduced contribution can offset this benefit. The project studies how channel choices and collaborative effort interact to determine responsibility outcomes.

Work in progress

Online Learning for Joint Pricing and Inventory Control with Reference Effects

Xinxiu Wang

Overview +

I study joint pricing and replenishment when customers compare current prices with an unobserved reference price formed from past prices. Both demand and the reference process are unknown. I develop policies that learn pricing and inventory decisions without explicitly estimating the reference process. The current work focuses on refining the theoretical analysis and the proofs of the performance guarantees.

02 / Teaching

Connecting models
with business decisions.

I help students understand why an analytical method is useful, how it works, and what its results mean in a business setting.

I introduce concepts through familiar examples and short videos, then move from intuitive explanations to worked problems and independent practice. I use office hours and review materials to support students with different levels of quantitative preparation.
